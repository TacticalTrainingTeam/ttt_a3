# Field Manual

This file mirrors the English field manual hint content in plain Markdown format.

## Infantry

### Basics

- **Always know about yourself and your buddy**
  - Position
  - Status (health, ammunition)
  - Viewing / security direction
- **Rules of engagement**
  - **RED**: hold fire
  - **YELLOW**: fire only when fired upon
  - **GREEN**: fire at will

### Basics - Radio

- **Rules**
  - Think, press, talk
  - Short and clear, no courtesy phrases
  - Wait for ongoing conversations
  - Whoever opens the call also closes it
- **Call sequence (German radio words)**
  - Call: "Grün-2, hier Grün-1, kommen"
  - Answer: "Hier Grün-2, kommen"
  - Message: "Hier Grün-1, [content], kommen"
  - Read back: "Hier Grün-2, so verstanden, [content], kommen"
  - Close: "Hier Grün-1, so richtig, Ende"
  - Other net: "Silber meldet sich an" ... "melde mich ab, Ende"
- **Prowords**
  - **so verstanden**: read-back follows
  - **so richtig / so bestätigt**: read-back correct
  - **negativ, ich wiederhole**: read-back wrong, message follows again
  - **Trennung**: next separate part
  - **ich korrigiere / annulliere**: correct or cancel a message
  - **Standby [x] Mike**: hold the call for x minutes
  - **Break, Break**: emergency, interrupts the net
  - **sprechen Sie zwomal**: bad signal, say everything twice
- **Contact report**
  - Contact | Direction | Type + number
  - Distance | Status (hot/cold) | Additional information
  - In a firefight: Direction | Type + number | Distance | Additional information
  - Example: "12 Uhr, vier Infanteristen, nah, bekämpfe"
- **Phonetic alphabet**
  - Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo, Foxtrot
  - Golf, Hotel, India, Juliett, Kilo, Lima
  - Mike, November, Oscar, Papa, Quebec, Romeo
  - Sierra, Tango, Uniform, Victor, Whiskey
  - X-Ray, Yankee, Zulu

### Colors and Markings

- **Smoke**
  - **Red**: enemy or danger
  - **Green**: own positions
  - **White**: screen the enemy
  - **Blue**: VSS / MRZ
  - **Orange**: JTAC only, CAS targets
  - Other colors: LZ marking by supply NCOs
- **Urban and trench combat**
  - **Red square, flag or chemlight**: entry point
  - **Green circle or chemlight**: building secured
  - **Blue diamond or flag**: VSS / medic needed
  - **Red X**: danger, do not enter
- **VSS / MRZ**
  - **Blue** flag, diamond, smoke or chemlight
  - Patient areas: **green** KAT Charlie, **yellow** KAT Bravo, **red** KAT Alpha
- **Engineers and EOD**
  - Mine lane: **yellow markers**, tips toward the safe center
  - No markers: **left red, right green**
  - Single explosive or UXO: **orange flag**, otherwise **red X** nearby
- **Supply and LZ**
  - Supply point: **yellow flag**
  - LZ: colored smoke or red signal panel
  - LZ at night: chemlight with white smoke

### First Aid

- **First**
  - **Protect yourself**, take cover and keep fighting until the threat is gone
  - **Report** the casualty, drag them to cover, use smoke
- **MARCH**
  - **M** Massive bleeding: tourniquets on arms and legs, bandage head and torso
  - **A** Airway: obstructed: overextend the head and stay close. Occluded: turn the head until cleared
  - **R** Respiration: chest seal on a deep chest wound
  - **C** Circulation: no pulse: CPR. Pulse but unconscious: wake (slap), otherwise recovery position
  - **H** Other: painkillers, watch the patient, call a medic
- **Triage**
  - **KAT ALPHA**: no pulse, treat immediately
  - **KAT BRAVO**: unable to fight, treat soon
  - **KAT CHARLIE**: lightly wounded, able to fight
- **Handover**
  - Who is the patient (name, role)?
  - Category
  - What happened?
  - How long unconscious?
  - What was done (tourniquets, medication)?

### Airborne Infantry

- **Preparation**
  - Mark **WP**, **DZ** and at least one **SZ** on the map
  - Backpack on chest, take parachute and altimeter (activate with O)
  - HALO/HAHO: **oxygen mask** required
  - Join the jump frequency without confirmation, squad leader reports mounted
  - Jumpmaster boards last and jumps last. **Nobody exits without a call**
- **Exit limits (above terrain)**
  - Plane: min **180m**, **200-250 km/h**
  - Helicopter: min **120m**, **120-150 km/h**
- **Jump types**
  - Systemsprung: up to 180m, open **immediately**
  - HALO: from 2500m, open by **500m** (reserve still possible), absolute minimum **250m**
  - HAHO: from 2500m, open **5 seconds** after exit
- **Landing**
  - From 50m: max **5 m/s**
  - At 20m: **3 m/s**
  - Touchdown: max **2 m/s**
- **Rally**
  - Report landing on the squad net
  - Reach the SZ within **5 minutes**
  - Search for missing: dead or unconscious **30 minutes**, alive until found
- **Chute failure**
  - Cut it via ACE self-interaction, open reserve (not steerable)

### Airmobile Infantry

- **At the helicopter**
  - Approach from the front, then arc to the side door
  - Keep clear of weapons. On slopes, watch the rotor
  - Follow crew orders, **the pilot has the last word**
- **On board**
  - Whisper volume only
  - Report threats to the pilot immediately
- **Before launch**
  - Clarify **where, what, how, who**
  - No FAC: No. 2 joins the crew's SR and reports all dismounted
- **LZ (FAC)**
  - Mark primary and secondary LZ
  - Send the 5-Liner, wait for read-back
  - Tell the pilot in advance if the LZ is hot
  - On approach: mark with smoke or light, guide on request
  - Comms lost: pilot joins the squad's SR
- **Dismount and lift-off**
  - After **Touchdown**, the squad leader commands. Lowest rank dismounts first
  - Hot LZ: normal boarding order is dropped
  - Lift-off when tasks are done or FAC reports complete
- **Fast-rope (ACE self-interaction)**
  - Prepare the system, deploy ropes, descend on command
  - Last man reports ground on the jump frequency, crew cuts the ropes

### Convoy

- **Always order**
  - **SP** Starting Point
  - **RP** Release Point: convoy leader dissolves the convoy when the last vehicle arrives
  - Vehicles of each team
  - Order of march, marked on the map as position + unit
  - Convoy frequency: **66**, always state it
  - Behavior on enemy contact
- **If needed**
  - **PP** Passing Points, numbered
  - **HP** Halt Points
  - Start time, distance to vanguard and rearguard
- **Default if not ordered**
  - **50 km/h, 50m** spacing
  - Planned halt: in formation at the roadside, 360 security while mounted
  - RP: 360 security from the vehicle
- **Spacing and speed**
  - Open convoy: max **100m**
  - Closed convoy: **25-50m**
  - Never under **5m**, keep sight of the vehicle ahead
  - Speed: 50-70 percent of the slowest vehicle's top speed
- **Forming up**
  - Vehicle leader moves to their place at the roadside
  - Delete the map marker and check in on the convoy frequency
- **Security on the move**
  - First vehicle ahead, last vehicle behind
  - Between them: right, then left, alternating
  - Only one security vehicle per end: front covers ahead and right, rear covers behind and left
- **At a halt**
  - **A halt is not parking**, be ready to move at once
  - On order to dismount: everyone except gunner and driver
  - Dismounted: secure in cover about **15m** from the vehicle, never use vehicles as cover

### Milan

- **Equipment**
  - **Milan Tripod**: launcher with thermal sight, about 30 kg
  - **Milan Tube**: one missile, about 12 kg
  - Spare tubes also in Marder and Wiesel TOW cargo
- **Setup**
  - Carry the **Milan Tripod** backpack, use **Assemble Milan** in the action menu
  - It stands empty, no tube needed
- **Load and unload**
  - Hold **Space for 10 seconds**, within 2m, not seated in it
  - **Load Milan**: Milan empty, **wear a Milan Tube** as backpack
  - **Unload Milan**: Milan loaded, **wear no backpack**
- **Firing**
  - Enter as gunner, the sight has **no rangefinder**
  - Empty after every shot, load a new tube
- **Pack up**
  - Milan **empty**, nobody in it, **no backpack** on you
  - **Disassemble Milan** in the action menu, the tripod becomes your backpack

### GraMaWa

- **Setup**
  - Two backpacks: **GMW for tripod** and **Tripod for GMW**
  - (1) One soldier places or carries the tripod
  - (2) A second soldier with the weapon builds it via the action menu
  - Pack up via the action menu
- **Loading (ACE interaction on the weapon)**
  - Belt: **32 rounds**
  - Load **10 seconds**, unload **8 seconds**
  - Fits: [CSW] 40mm GMG Belt, GMW 32 rounds belt
  - **GMW ammo box** in backpack: use **Reload** on the empty weapon
- **Elevation and traverse**
  - Outside: ACE interaction on the weapon
  - As gunner: ACE self-interaction
  - **Adjust height** (Up, Down), **Turn** (Left, Right)

### MG3 Tripod

- **Requirements**
  - Backpack **Tripod for MG3**
  - An MG3 **from BWMod**, others do not fit
- **Setup**
  - (1) Build the tripod via the action menu
  - (2) Hold the MG3 as active weapon
  - (3) ACE interaction: **Mount MG3**, takes 5 seconds
  - Empty tripod: drag, carry or **Pickup Tripod**
- **Reload (ACE interaction on the MG)**
  - Load **5 seconds**, unload **4 seconds**, up to **250 rounds**
  - Fits: MG3 100 rounds belt, 150-round 7.62mm boxes, BWMod 120-round belts
- **Elevation and traverse**
  - Outside: ACE interaction on the tripod
  - As gunner: ACE self-interaction
  - **Adjust height** (Up, Down), **Turn** (Left, Right)
  - The MG3 can overheat on the tripod
- **Pack up**
  - ACE interaction: **Disassemble MG3 Tripod**, nobody on the gun, 10 seconds
  - MG3 back in hand, ammo in inventory, tripod on the ground as backpack

  - Rear ramp closed: only driver and commander can board
  - Open or close: **driver, gunner or center hatch** (action menu)
  - From outside: ask the crew via the **infantry phone**, rear left
  - Turned out: **Get out through hatch** works with the ramp closed
  - Commander's seat stuck: use **Fix get in seat**
- **Hatch sectors**
  - Left: 7-11 o'clock
  - Right: 1-5 o'clock
  - Center: 5-7 o'clock, usually the team leader
- **Autocannon**
  - **AP** against vehicles, **HE** against infantry and soft targets
  - Switch via normal weapon selection, zeroed to 800m at spawn
- **Smoke**
  - Gunner or commander: **Countermeasures** key
  - Salvos shown as **NbMWAnl: x**, red means not ready
  - Needs a living, conscious gunner
- **Milan (hold Space for 10 seconds)**

  - (2) Commander or right hatch: **Load Milan**
  - (3) Commander: **Get In Milan**, fire from a halt, it is not stabilized
  - (4) Reload after every shot, four tubes in cargo
  - (5) To pack up: empty, nobody on it, commander uses **Disassemble Milan**
- **Radio**
  - Rack 1: SEM90 + SEM70 (net A)
  - Rack 2: PRC103 + PRC117F (net B)

## Combat Support

### JTAC

- **Sequence**
  - (1) Set IPs and BPs
  - (2) Open channel with the aircraft
  - (3) Check-in: type, position, weapons, **TOS**
  - (4) Aircraft holds at the **Contact Point**
  - (5) SITREP: friendlies and enemy
  - (6) Attack type and **9-Liner**
  - (7) **BDA**
  - (8) Reattack if needed
- **Attack type**
  - **Type 1** (default): JTAC and aircraft see the target and each other
  - **Type 2**: at least one line of sight is broken
  - **Type 3**: as type 2, aircraft engages freely within the given AO
- **9-Liner**
  - (1) IP/BP
  - (2) Heading
  - (3) Distance
  - (4) Elevation
  - (5) Description
  - (6) Location: 6-digit, 8-digit if needed
  - (7) Type Mark: laser, smoke, none
  - (8) Friendlies: **Danger Close** requires green smoke, none near: **No Factor**
  - (9) Egress
  - Remarks: weapon restrictions
- **Attack calls**
  - Pilot: **Inbound**, JTAC: **Continue / Abort**
  - Pilot: **Spot / No Spot** (laser acquired or not)
  - Pilot: **Tally / No Joy** (target in sight or not)
  - JTAC: **Cleared Hot**, then **Hit / Miss**
  - No Cleared Hot, No Spot or No Joy: **no release**, break off via egress

### Forward Observer

- **Before the mission**
  - FO and guns agree on **one coordinate and elevation source** (map or GPS)
  - Confirm friendly positions around the target
- **FDCB**
  - (0) Number: one FO and one battery: FO, otherwise FDC
  - (1) Type: **I** when ready, **II** on command, **III** at a set time (time in line 7)
  - (2) Attack: point, line, area. Under 50m radius or 50-100m length is a point attack
  - (3) Coordinate: **min. 8-digit**, better 10-digit
  - (4) Elevation (ASL), mean height for line or area
  - (5) Round count
  - (6) Ammunition, can be combined with line 5: "3 HE, 3 Annäherung"
  - (7) Remarks: "Einschläge alle 30 Sekunden", "erst nach Auftrag 1"
- **Ammunition**
  - **HE**: fragmentation on impact
  - **Proximity**: airburst against infantry and soft vehicles
  - **Smoke**: screen
  - **Illumination**: battlefield light
- **Radio**
  - "Bereit für Feuerauftrag?" - "Bereit!" - FDCB - read-back
  - FDC: **ETA [x]** when firing, **Fire complete** after the last round, **Splash** 5 seconds before impact
  - Repeat by number: "Auftrag 5 wiederholen"
  - After impact: report effect, request Re-Attack, update the guns

### Sniper Team

- **Position**
  - Shoot from **3-5m behind** a window with the SSWT kit, never at the window
  - In woods use corridors between trees, **never the tree line**
  - Plan infil and exfil away from roads and open ground
- **Naming and DOPE**
  - Name areas and targets: Spot 1, Haus 1, Target 1
  - DOPE per target: "Target 1, Range 1854, Azi 045, Chill, 11.6 clicks"
  - Moving target: range a static object next to it
- **Fire sequence**
  - (1) Spotter: **Set on** [target]
  - (2) Shooter: **On Glas**
  - (3) Shooter ready: **Kindle**
  - (4) Spotter: **Fire, Fire, Fire**, shot on the last Fire
  - (5) **Hit / Miss / Mrs.** (missed, keep firing), **Cute** (eliminated), **Deer** (wounded)
- **Corrections**
  - **Hold Off**: aim higher
  - **Hold Over**: aim lower
  - **Hold Left / Right**: horizontal
- **Shot types**
  - **Ghost**: one shot
  - **Shadow**: two shots
  - **Choir**: several shooters on Fire
  - **Knockout**: second shot by another shooter
  - **Crash**: spotter distracts with fire
- **Status**
  - Situation: **Cozy** safe, **Hot** possible counter-recon, **Burn** detected
  - Target: **Chill** passive, **Cold** active, **Crazy** aggressive
  - Team: **Satellite** stays, **Date** moves on, **Choke** withdraws fast

### Combat Engineer

- **Manual lane**
  - Enemy minefields are always **observed**: secure and screen first
  - (1) Squad leader sets start and end with the platoon leader
  - (2) Line with about 2m spacing, about 2m lane width per engineer
  - (3) On command, search ahead and about 30 degrees to each side
  - (4) Mine found: report **immediately** on the squad net
  - (5) Everyone else **stops and goes prone**
  - (6) Finder disarms, take the mine along or collect it at a set point
  - (7) Squad leader directs and marks lane edges on the map in yellow
- **Mark the lane**
  - **Yellow markers** left and right, tips point to the safe center
  - Spacing per side: 1-2m
  - No markers: spray paint, **left red, right green**
- **Detector and disarming**
  - Pistol slot, **activate after every weapon change**
  - Range 2m, 90 degrees ahead. Signal is delayed
  - Defusal kit, crawl up with **empty hands**, ACE interaction
- **Mines**
  - **M15 AT** (10m): disarm. Only several large-caliber hits, no clearing charge
  - **VS-50 AP** (15m): disarm, shoot or blast
  - **M26 bouncing** (100m): **never disarm**, shoot or blast
  - **M4A1 SLAM** (100m forward): **never disarm**, shoot from behind or blast
  - **PMR-3 tripwire** (35m): disarm, shoot or blast
  - **M18A1 Claymore** (100m forward, 50m sides and rear): disarm from behind, shoot or blast
- **MICLIC**
  - Place at the edge so "Explosive" reads normally for the person firing
  - Hold **Space about 5 seconds**
  - **45 seconds - 45 meters**, into hard cover
  - Clears **70m x 4m**. Mark both sides, no extra sweep

### Laying a Minefield

- **A minefield is always**
  - **Limited** in time and space
  - **Observed**
  - **Documented** and shared with own forces
- **Before laying**
  - Engineer team leader recons the ground and sets the corner points
  - Lay mines **only on the squad leader's order**
- **Mixed minefield**
  - (1) Moving toward the enemy: **AT mines** in a zigzag, about 5m apart
  - (2) Moving back: fill the gaps with an **AP zigzag**
- **Afterwards**
  - Mark it **red** on the map in side channel
  - Place the own-minefield marker with mine types at the edge
  - Keep it under observation
  - Reinforce with barbed wire whenever possible
- **Lane**
  - Plan a lane if friendly forces may need to withdraw
  - Close it with a prepared **security minefield**, usually one row of AT mines

### Demolition

- **Arming (ACE)**
  - (1) Self-interaction: Explosives, select charge
  - (2) Place with left mouse button
  - (3) Interaction on the charge: select detonator
  - (4) Timer: set the time. Clacker or phone: note the code
  - (5) Fire: self-interaction, Explosives, detonator
- **Sequence**
  - (1) Lay all charges
  - (2) Clear **blast radius plus 5m** before attaching detonators
  - (3) One engineer per object attaches all its detonators
  - (4) Report ready to squad leader, who reports to platoon leader
- **Radio (German)**
  - "Hier Blau, bereit zur Sprengung, kommen"
  - "Hier Gelb, verstanden, Trennung, Sprengung in T-5 Mike, kommen"
  - "Hier Blau, verstanden, Sprengung in T-5 Mike, Blau Ende"
  - To all: "Sprengung in T-10, 9 ... 1, SPRENGUNG"
  - Fire on **SPRENGUNG**
  - Report success. If failed, report and repeat on order

### EOD

- **5 C's**
  - (1) **Confirm** with a detector
  - (2) **Clear** away from fragments and secondary blasts
  - (3) **Call** EOD
  - (4) **Cordon** off people and vehicles
  - (5) **Control** until EOD reports "Bereich sicher"
- **Marking**
  - **Orange flag** near the find
  - No flag: **red X** in spray paint
- **5-0-25 at vehicle halts**
  - **5m:** crew checks while mounted, at every halt
  - **0m:** trained soldier checks vehicle and ground beneath
  - **25m:** same soldier searches the radius
  - From three minutes or when infantry dismounts: all three
- **Decision (EOD leader with OPL)**
  - Disarm, move, blast, shoot or bypass
  - Report "alles sicher" only when nothing else is found
- **Disarm and clear**
  - Defusal kit, crawl up with **empty hands**, ACE interaction
  - Pick up via mouse wheel, do **not** re-arm by accident
  - Clearing: **nobody in blast or fragment radius**
  - M183 satchel charges cannot be set off by gunfire
- **IEDs**
  - Basic: small 15m, large 20m, remote trigger possible, prefer clearing by gunfire
  - Advanced: reacts to movement within 5-10m, keep **150m** safety distance
  - Defuser describes, instructor finds it in the notebook, **both must be sure**
  - Name wires one at a time, "lang" before the color
  - Not in the notebook: **cannot be disarmed**
  - Advanced: clear with an **M112**, gunfire needs 12.7mm or larger
- **ED-1D**
  - Radar: 50m
  - Clear with **slugs**, keep at least **20m** from the explosive
  - Advanced IEDs: clear with an **M112** instead
  - May drive over anti-personnel mines, **never** over IEDs or SLAM

### Drones

- **UAV terminal**
  - Goes in the **GPS slot**, open via the action menu
  - Map: right-click the drone, **connect terminal to UAV**
  - One terminal per drone, one operator per drone
  - Terminals are **side-bound**
  - Take over as driver/pilot or gunner
- **Before launch**
  - Set a high **Client View Distance** in ACE options
  - Pack spare **UAV batteries**
  - Raven uses its own ground control station, not the terminal
- **Battery (ACE)**
  - Small quadcopters such as the Darter have a battery
  - Recharge via interaction with a UAV battery
- **In use**
  - TTT link addon active: image degrades with distance, then drops
  - Recon only: **forbid firing** in the terminal, armed drones engage on their own
  - Agree laser code and timing with the shooter first
  - Report sightings immediately as a contact report

### Electronic Warfare

- **Rules**
  - Weigh every jam: it can restrict friendlies and reveal you
  - Own signature: strict radio discipline, lead by voice where possible
  - Always cross-check results with other sources
  - Locate a source: measure from several positions and map the bearings
- **Spectrum Device antennas**
  - **Military:** 30-513 MHz, recon and listening, includes drone control
  - **Experimental:** 520-1090 MHz, signals and trackers
  - **Jamming:** 433-445 MHz, detect and jam drones
- **Controls**
  - **Mouse wheel**: operate
  - **Ctrl+wheel**: zoom
  - **Shift+wheel**: move spectrum when zoomed
  - **Space**: bearing line on your map (needs a vanilla GPS in the GPS slot)
- **Jammers**
  - Broadband jammer: peak sweeping from low to high frequency
  - **Effective radius**: affected devices useless
  - **Falloff radius**: effect fades with distance
  - Drones: video artifacts up to loss of connection
- **Special**
  - **SATCOM**: radios in its radius cannot be jammed
  - **EMP**: destroys electronics in its radius and removes them from inventory

## Logistics

### Logistics

- **Principles**
  - Self-protection first
  - **People before material**
  - No one is left behind
  - Logistics avoids all combat
- **5-Liner**
  - (0) Number
  - (1) Location: fixed name, new place means new name
  - (2) Heading
  - (3) Receiver: secures and sets up the LZ/VP
  - (4) Task
  - (5) Marking and remarks
- **Radio**
  - "Bereit für 5-Liner, kommen"
  - Send with **pauses**, require **read-back**, clarify gaps
  - Report approach early
  - After handover: report completion and remaining stock
- **Crate codes**
  - VBT, VBA
  - Inf Mun, Inf Mun Schwer
  - Werfer, Gren, Fhz Mun
- **Delivery**
  - Ground: handover, remain or staging
  - Air: handover only, minimal ground time, **abort if LZ is not ready**
  - Mixed: air to LZ, then ground to VP
- **Rank on board (highest first)**
  - Logistics officer, pilot/driver, crew chief, co-pilot
  - Gunner, logistician/medic, OPL, infantry

### Sling Load

- **Planning**
  - Choose LZ and an unused SR: "LZ Backyard SR131", optional IP
  - 5-Liner, SR and IP under remarks
  - LZ at least the size of the load, flat, free of obstacles
  - Approach uphill if possible, steady **5-10 degree** descent
  - No troops or vehicles in the LZ
  - Loadmaster: co-pilot side at 45 degrees, **5-20m behind** the LZ, never cross the approach axis
- **Responsibility**
  - Pilot until checking in on approach
  - **Loadmaster** from then until **Clear out**
  - Pilot may abort at any time
- **Radio (English)**
  - Pilot: "LZ Backyard, Packhorse-1, Inbound IP Sparrow"
  - LM: "Packhorse-1, LZ Backyard, Clear in!"
  - Pilot: "Clear in, Packhorse-1", then no more acknowledgements
  - End: "Load on ground, Release!" - "Load is free, up, clear out!"
- **Standard voice**
  - Move: Forward, Back, Left, Right, Up, Down, Turn Left/Right, Fly straight
  - Height: Height is [m], Height is good, Height above Obstacle, Obstacle past
  - Load: Load on, Load is on, Load on ground, Load clear of ground, Release, Load is free
  - Rope: Strope is coming tight
  - Correction, Gently
- **Numbers and height**
  - Digit by digit in meters: "Forward One-Two-Five"
  - 4 = Fourer, 9 = Niner
  - Height before distance, reference is the load
  - Bad approach: go around
